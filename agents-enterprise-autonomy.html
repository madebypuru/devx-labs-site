    Questions

    Questions buyers ask

    Will agents replace my team?

    The work changes first. We redesign roles with the people doing them, measure the capacity released and agree with you where it goes: more cases, harder cases or new work.

    What if an agent makes a mistake in our ERP?

    It can, which is why high-impact actions need approval, actions are reversible where possible, limits are set per tool, and every step is logged.

    Low-code or high-code?

    Low-code when the task is bounded and the business wants to own it. High-code when it crosses many systems or has expensive failure modes. Most clients run both on one platform.

    What happens when the underlying model changes?

    The Assurance practice runs every agent’s eval suite on the new model before any switch.

    Show us the process your best people hate doing.

    We will show you the agent that does it, running on your data, with your approvals in place.
